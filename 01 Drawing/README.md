Very simply a rendition of the ":3" emoticon as an emoji, popular within the transgender community for its playfulness and association with catgirl culture. Often used by trans women to convey "gender euphoria", the positive counterpart to gender dysphoria - the feeling of alignment with your previously suppressed gender. :3 is also commonly used in online communities as a way for trans people to identify each other. The design uses simple geometry to show how a small, familiar symbol can carry a more complex social meaning within certain circles.
