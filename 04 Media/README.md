Unknown Artist - Pointing Wojaks

Originally an image of two men pointing at a "Beyond Fried Chicken" sign, the "pointing Wojaks" have become a meme used to display allegiance to or recognition of fandom media, references, and interests, among other things. I used a fan-edited version of the final battle scene from Avengers: Endgame as a good source of recognisable references and characters. Viewers click to start the video, and then hold the mouse when they see something familiar, triggering the pointing Wojak figures and a loud scream, exaggerating their reaction.

Many modern films, especially Endgame and others in the Marvel franchise, are nostalgic spectacles of references and in-jokes, perhaps to the detriment of the films themselves, and I wanted to make fun of this. By allowing the viewer to trigger this reaction at their discretion, I wanted to make them a part of the phenomenon I am criticising.
