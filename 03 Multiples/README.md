This code simulates an exaggerated version of terms and conditions agreements that exist on various websites. In this version, clicking on one box to agree to its condition spawns two more conditions that must be accepted to continue, forming an infinite loop from which the user cannot escape. As more conditions are accepted, the new ones start to become slightly disturbing, with the user needing to sign away more and more of their rights to continue. These agreements on websites are often checked without us even considering what they mean, which I wanted to explore with this piece.
